Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

THE WEATHER POINT · Effective 18 July 2026

THE WEATHER POINT (“the Company”, “the Service”) is an HVAC contractor specializing in central and split AC fitting, mechanical engineering site surveys, and climate control installations. We only process information required to evaluate building cooling specs and schedule engineering site visits; we send no marketing or promotional spam.

Information We Process

  • The text of cooling requests, square footage, and building specs you submit.
  • Your contact name, phone number, and email address to coordinate site visits.
  • Property details (room height, sun exposure, building usage type).
  • We do not collect location tracking, contacts, media, or financial payment details.

How We Use It

Engineering requests are processed on our secure company dispatch server and logged into a private database controlled by THE WEATHER POINT. Project data is strictly used to compute cooling tonnage requirements and prepare official HVAC fitting estimates.

Storage & Sharing

Data is stored in encrypted databases. Access is strictly restricted to certified company engineers and authorized project managers. We do not sell or share your personal details with third parties or external advertisers.

Retention & Deletion

Site inspection records remain stored until deleted by the operator upon project completion or client request. Contact us at privacy@theweatherpoint.online to access, modify, or permanently delete your data.
